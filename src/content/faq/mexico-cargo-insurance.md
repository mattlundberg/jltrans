Under Mexican federal law, all Mexican carriers are limited in cargo loss liability to **$0.05 per
kilogram**.

Because of that limit, most shippers either self-insure or purchase Inland Marine Insurance policies
at their own cost. Mexican insurance companies will sell cargo policies at roughly 1–2% of cargo
value.
