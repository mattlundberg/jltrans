question: What document requirements are needed to move products into Mexico?
summary: >-
  All shipments into or out of Mexico require a customs broker. Requirements vary by export or
  import, whether the shipment is in bond, and the type of product.
order: 4
---

All shipments into or out of Mexico require the services of a customs broker. Their expertise
ensures the proper documents are prepared, which avoids delays to your shipment.

Requirements vary depending on whether the shipment is an export or an import, whether it moves in
bond, and the type of product involved. A broker can also direct the payment of any taxes or duties
assessed by the various governments.
