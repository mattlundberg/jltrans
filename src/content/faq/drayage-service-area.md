J&L Transportation serves all three intermodal ramps in Arizona:

- BNSF Phoenix
- UPRR Phoenix
- UPRR Tucson

We provide dependable and economical drayage service to all of Arizona and most of Sonora, Mexico,
as well as Northern Nevada from our Sparks, Nevada service center.
