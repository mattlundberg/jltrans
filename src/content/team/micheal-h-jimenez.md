Prior to starting J&L Transportation, Mike was involved in the trucking business as a driver,
mechanic, and owner since 1972. He is a noted authority on intermodal and drayage operations in
the western U.S., and is often called upon by the industry for advice and counsel.

Mr. Jimenez served as Chairman of the Arizona Trucking Association from 2001 to 2003 and is a
member of its Board of Directors and Executive Committee. He currently serves as Arizona's State
Vice President to the American Trucking Associations.
