Having grown up in the business, there is little that Michael has not experienced around J&L
Transportation. He started in the shop painting wheels while still in high school, then worked
dispatch for five years. Today he handles day-to-day operations.

Michael completed his studies in business management at Glendale Community College. He also
enjoys racing, having driven the J&L racing kart in competitions across Arizona, California, and
Nevada, winning numerous titles.
